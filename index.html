How this works

  • Prices and rents are the Zillow Home Value Index (middle-tier single-family homes and condos) and the Zillow Observed Rent Index (all rental types, including apartments), metro level, September 2026.
  • Gross yield is typical monthly rent × 12 ÷ typical home value. It leaves out taxes, insurance, vacancy, repairs and financing.
  • Population is the Census Bureau's Vintage 2025 metro estimate, compared with 2020.
  • Catalysts and laws come from the reporting and official sources linked on each market page. A market with nothing listed may still have news; we add items once they are confirmed.
  • Which markets: metros of roughly 200,000 people or more where the typical home is near or below $350K, with at least one of above-average yield, above-average rent growth, or a major employer or project. Two markets, Grand Rapids and Springfield, sit a little above that price line and stay on the list as growth comparisons.
  • Updates: the figures refresh monthly when Zillow publishes new data. Job growth from the Bureau of Labor Statistics is coming in a later update.

Overlooked Markets publishes general market information for education. It is not investment, legal or tax advice, and it is not an offer to buy or sell any security or property. Metro figures are averages that hide large differences between neighborhoods. Check any property's taxes, insurance and condition yourself.

Data: Zillow Home Value Index and Zillow Observed Rent Index (Zillow Research), U.S. Census Bureau population estimates (Vintage 2025), and the reporting linked on each market page.
